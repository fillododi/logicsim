# Register12

Remembers a 12-bit number. Each clock cycle, if `load` is 1, `out` becomes `in`; otherwise it keeps its value. The 4004's program counter, and each level of its address stack, is 12 bits wide.

**Parts you may use:** Nand, Register4, Register8.

**Hint:** Split the 12 bits between registers you already have, and give every one the same load.
