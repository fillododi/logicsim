# RAM4002

The 4004's data memory comes in 4002 chips. Each holds 4 registers, and each register has 16 main characters and 4 status characters, all 4 bits.

- `addr[4..5]` picks the register.
- With `status` 0, `addr[0..3]` picks one of its 16 main characters.
- With `status` 1, `addr[0..1]` picks one of its 4 status characters, and `addr[2..3]` doesn't matter.

`out` is the character picked, and `load` stores `in` there at the next clock cycle. Main and status characters are separate: writing one never changes the other.

In the 4004, `SRC` picks the register and main character, and `WRM`/`RDM` write and read it; `WR0`–`WR3` and `RD0`–`RD3` reach that register's status characters.

**Parts you may use:** Nand, DMux, Mux4, RAM8, RAM64.

**Hint:** The 64 main characters fit one RAM64 just as they are numbered. The 16 status characters fit two RAM8s, so a RAM64 for them would waste 48. A pin can be wired a few bits at a time: `Foo(sel[0..1]=x, sel[2]=y)`.
