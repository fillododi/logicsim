# Register4

Remembers a 4-bit number. Each clock cycle, if `load` is 1, `out` becomes `in`; otherwise it keeps its value. The 4004 keeps its accumulator and each of its 16 index registers in 4 bits.

**Parts you may use:** Nand, Bit.

**Hint:** Each bit of a register is stored on its own, and they all load together.
