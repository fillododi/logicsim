# Mux4Way12

Mux4Way4, 12 bits wide. The 4004's next address comes from one of a few places (the next instruction, a jump, the stack), and this picks one.

| sel | out |
|---|---|
| 0 | a |
| 1 | b |
| 2 | c |
| 3 | d |

**Parts you may use:** Nand, Mux12.

**Hint:** The same shape as Mux4Way4, with wider parts.
